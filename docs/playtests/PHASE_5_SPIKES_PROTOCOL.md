Status: PREPARED; sessions and deployed-build verification NOT TESTED.

Use approximately five computing/strategy-game target users, one recorded build and traffic-spikes v1. Record fresh/returning participant status, recruited/organic source, company/run ID, session ID, build/commit, seed, inherited read/write profile and entry architecture/cash. Do not apply account/cloud drafts or claim browser-local exports are uploaded.

## Preparation

- Use the same company after Data Strategy, or an explicitly recorded engine-generated same-company fixture. Fixture history is setup, not participant achievement/replay.
- Keep pulse offsets +8/+28/+48/+68 and 2,400/4,000 demand identical across comparisons.
- Record controller settings, workload/cache state, traffic limit and available cash. Do not give a failing insolvent fixture as a scaling lesson.
- Verify local guest start/resume/export with API unavailable and record the actual deployed build ID. A dev/unrecorded build is not fixed-build participant evidence.
- Explain only navigation and pause controls; do not prescribe autoscaling, manual planning, cache or DB investment.

## Post-play questions

1. Why did automatic scaling not react instantly?
2. Why did an instance appear before receiving traffic?
3. What evidence shows whether automation helped?
4. What was constrained: application, database, or both?
5. Why does extra capacity cost money after demand falls?
6. When might manual preparation or traffic limiting be preferable?

Primary validation question: can the participant explain delayed, app-only automation and its capacity/cost trade-off using actual evidence?

After enjoyment/spontaneous-interest recording, ask whether they want to continue or try another run. Prompted agreement is not spontaneous demand. A counterbalanced manual/automatic comparison is prompted evaluation, not voluntary replay. Record prevention/failure as valid observations; do not manufacture incidents.

Export the existing playtest record and current company evidence before explicit reset. Keep unexported records; a download alone does not authorize cleanup. Record manual notes against the session, never infer facilitator intervention from behavior. Do not conduct sessions, recruit participants or claim learning effectiveness automatically. This protocol is not the later learning-evaluation cohort.
