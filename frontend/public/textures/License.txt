Photo-scanned surface textures

License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0)
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects; credit is not
required. The original 1K sets were downscaled and recompressed for the web.
Sets the game tints were turned grey. Every folder holds color.jpg, normal.jpg
(OpenGL) and arm.jpg (R = ambient occlusion, G = roughness, B = metalness).

The city's ground and roofs use five more Poly Haven sets, also CC0. Their colour
maps were turned grey and downscaled to 768 px, their normal maps to 512 px, and
they hold only color.jpg and normal.jpg; the city has no use for an arm map.
