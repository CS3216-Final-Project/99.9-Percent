Free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects; credit is not
required. Downloaded as GLB from kenney.nl/assets/cube-pets and compressed
with gltf-transform (meshopt). The player's avatars are the 18 animals the
office crew do not already have: beaver, caterpillar, cow, crab, deer, dog,
elephant, fish, fox, giraffe, hog, koala, lion, panda, penguin, pig, polar
(bear) and tiger. They share one texture, Textures/colormap.png. The 64 px
pictures in previews/ are Kenney's own previews from the same pack.
